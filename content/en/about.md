I'm a cybersecurity engineer based in France. Double degree — MSc in IT Reliability & Security from Aix-Marseille University, State Engineering Degree in Cybersecurity from ENSA Oujda.

What got me into low-level security is simple: it's one of the few areas where you actually have to think hard, and when something clicks, you know you've understood it for real. There's a kind of satisfaction in figuring out exactly how something breaks — not at an abstract level, but at the instruction level, the byte level. That's what keeps me going.
