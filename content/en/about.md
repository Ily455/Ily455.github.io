Cybersecurity engineer based in Marseille, France.

I graduated with an MSc in IT Reliability & Security from Aix-Marseille University and a State Engineering Degree in Cybersecurity from ENSA Oujda.

My background spans malware analysis, R&D in embedded security, and governance. I am particularly drawn to low-level security — reverse engineering, vulnerability research, and anything that requires understanding how things work at the hardware and compiler level. I like reading papers, building small tools, and figuring out why things break.

- **Network security:** SSL/TLS, PKI, VPN, Wireshark, PfSense, OPNsense, iptables
- **Systems & virtualization:** Linux, Windows, ESXi, Docker, QEMU, VirtualBox
- **Programming & scripting:** Python, C, Bash, SQL, Java, Git
- **GRC:** ISO 27001, ISO 27002, NIST CSF v2, EBIOS RM, OWASP SAMM
- **Languages:** Arabic (native), French (fluent), English (fluent)
