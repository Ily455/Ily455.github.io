A hands-on project focused on building and documenting a secure enterprise-style network architecture on ESXi.

## Scope

- Routing and switching design across segmented network zones
- Firewalling with PfSense/OPNsense
- Core services setup: DNS, DHCP, VPN (IPSec/OpenVPN), and DMZ exposure
- Monitoring rollout with Zabbix
- End-to-end technical documentation: architecture file, test plans, and operating procedures

## What I learned

- How to translate security requirements into practical network segmentation
- Tradeoffs between accessibility and hardening in multi-zone designs
- The importance of documentation quality for operations and audit readiness
